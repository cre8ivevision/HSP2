# 01. Pakistan Hosting Market Landscape

Status: Research
Last updated: 2026-09-21

## Objective

Understand the structure of the Pakistan-facing hosting market and identify conditions under which a small new provider could enter without prematurely building a large infrastructure operation.

## Market structure

The market can be viewed as:

1. Domain registration
2. Shared hosting
3. WordPress/application-optimized hosting
4. Business/managed hosting
5. Reseller and agency hosting
6. VPS/cloud
7. Dedicated infrastructure
8. Adjacent services such as email, SSL, backups, security, and migrations

Local providers commonly bundle several of these into one customer relationship.

## Initial hypotheses

- Competing on raw storage/bandwidth price alone may be difficult to sustain.
- Customer experience, support, migration assistance, and transparent service boundaries may provide differentiation.
- A focused product range may be operationally safer than launching every hosting category simultaneously.
- Agency/freelancer customers may provide a path to multiple end-customer accounts per relationship.
- Managed services can increase revenue per customer but also increase support workload and operational risk.
