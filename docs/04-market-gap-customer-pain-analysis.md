# 04. Market Gap & Customer Pain Analysis

Status: Research / hypothesis validation
Last updated: 2026-09-21

## Objective

Identify customer problems that could form the basis of a differentiated Pakistan-focused hosting business.

## Evidence signals

## Customer pain hypotheses

### A. Choosing the right plan

Customers may struggle to translate technical limits into actual website requirements.

Validation: interview first-time buyers and freelancers and observe plan-selection journeys.

### B. Reliability uncertainty

Customers may have difficulty distinguishing advertised uptime from independently measured reliability.

Validation: build a standardized uptime/performance measurement program.

### C. Support quality

Hosting support can involve a gray boundary between server support and website support.

Validation: collect support test cases and classify what providers will and will not solve.

### D. Migration anxiety

Moving a live website, email, DNS, and databases creates operational risk.

Validation: map migration steps and measure provider assistance.

### E. Renewal-price surprise

Introductory pricing can make long-term costs difficult to compare.

Validation: record first-term and renewal pricing for major plans.

### F. Payment friction

Pakistan-focused customers may expect local banks and wallets in addition to cards.

Validation: benchmark payment methods, confirmation time, reconciliation, refunds, and recurring-payment capability.

### G. Fragmented digital services

A small business may need domain, hosting, email, SSL, backups, and website help but may not know which vendor should own each layer.

Validation: interview SMEs, freelancers, and agencies.

## Opportunity hypotheses

Potential opportunity areas:

- Plain-language hosting plans
- Transparent renewal pricing
- Local payment convenience
- Strong migration service
- Human support with explicit scope
- Managed WordPress for non-technical businesses
- Agency/freelancer hosting with client management
- Automated backups and recovery
- Service-status transparency
- Developer-friendly hosting without enterprise complexity

These are hypotheses, not approved product decisions.

## Validation rule

Do not build the company around a complaint found in one forum thread. Use community discussions to generate hypotheses, then validate them with broader evidence.
