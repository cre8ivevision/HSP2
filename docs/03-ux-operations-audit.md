# 03. UX & Operations Audit

Status: Research
Last updated: 2026-09-21

## Objective

Study hosting as an end-to-end service rather than a server specification.

## Customer journey

Discover → Compare → Select → Pay → Provision → Launch → Manage → Get Help → Renew → Upgrade/Migrate/Cancel

Every stage creates both a UX requirement and an operational requirement.

## Audit areas

### Plan discovery

- Can a first-time buyer understand which plan they need?
- Are resources explained in customer language?
- Are renewal prices visible?
- Are important limits disclosed before checkout?
- Is there a clear upgrade path?

### Checkout

- Is pricing transparent?
- Are taxes and fees clear?
- Are local payment methods available?
- Can customers complete payment without manual intervention?
- How quickly is service provisioned after payment?

### Client portal

A future Hostable.pk portal should be evaluated around services, domains, billing, payments, support, usage, backups, security, DNS, credentials, renewals, upgrades, and service status.

### Support operations

Support should be modeled as an operational system.

Potential support classes:

1. Billing
2. Domain
3. Email
4. Website/application
5. Hosting configuration
6. Security
7. Migration
8. Performance
9. Outage

Each class can have its own SLA, escalation path, and automation.

## Hostable.pk operational hypotheses

- Automate routine billing and provisioning.
- Keep humans available for exceptions and complex issues.
- Make service status visible.
- Make migration a repeatable process.
- Keep support history attached to the customer/service.
- Make renewal dates and prices obvious.
- Define support boundaries clearly.
